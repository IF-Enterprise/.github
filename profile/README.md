## Our Projects

### 1. Points-Counter

**A voice-enabled points counter for Android and Wear OS.**

Points-Counter is a mobile application designed to make scorekeeping easier through voice commands and a straightforward user interface. It allows users to manage points during games without having to track every score manually.

**Key Features**
- Voice-controlled scorekeeping.
- Multilingual speech recognition.
- Support for Android smartphones and Wear OS smartwatches.
- Game management and score tracking.
- Save and load functionality for supported games.
- Customizable settings.

**Technologies:** Kotlin, Android SDK, Vosk Speech Recognition  
**Platforms:** Android and Wear OS  
**License:** GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)

### 2. Jutges Calendar

**A judicial staff scheduling and substitution management application.**

Jutges Calendar is a desktop application developed for the Department of Justice of the Government of Catalonia (*Generalitat de Catalunya*). It simplifies the management of judicial staff, court assignments, holidays, and substitution scheduling.

**Key Features**
- Employee and court management.
- Employee availability and holiday registration.
- Automated substitute selection based on workload points and availability.
- Court preferences and proximity considerations when selecting substitutes.
- Fair distribution of multiple substitutions across available employees.
- Excel (`.xlsx`) export for data analysis and record keeping.

**Platform:** Windows 10/11  
**License:** GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)

The application aims to streamline administrative workflows and improve the efficiency and fairness of judicial staff allocation.
